The Neo Geo CD version: all ten puzzles on a real disc.

- **Neo Geo CD disc**: the whole game rewritten in C for the Neo Geo CD. Download `Crossmix-v0.10.0-NeoGeoCD-BINCUE.zip` (burn it, or use an optical drive emulator, MiSTer or an emulator) or `Crossmix-v0.10.0-NeoGeoCD.chd` (MAME).
- All ten puzzles: Brick, Calm, Climb, Jewel, Swap, Pop, Grab, Nom Nom! Pets, Arsenal and Power Brick, each with a CPU player (levels 1-8).
- Modes: Versus CPU, Versus 2P, Survival and Side by Side, best of 3. Leave the title screen alone for 20 s for a CPU demo. Start pauses.
- Arsenal weapons and Power Brick powers (Fog, Swap Hands, Rush, Shield, Shake) work against every puzzle, just like the PC version.
- Small changes to fit the Neo Geo screen: Jewel's well is 12 rows, Grab's field 11 rows, Pop's bubbles are 16 px. No LAN play on the Neo Geo.
- In the busiest pairings (Swap or Pop against another heavy puzzle) the screen updates a little less smoothly, but the game always runs at the right speed.
- The PC version (Windows, Linux, browser) is unchanged from v0.9.0 apart from this release's build.

Power Brick: all ten puzzles are now in.

- **Power Brick** (Tetris Battle Gaiden style): about 1 piece in 4 carries a pink crystal. Clearing lines fills your power meter (crystals fill it faster); every 4 points is a star, up to 5. Press **C** to cast the power that matches your stars (it spends them all, so saving up gets you a stronger one):
  1 SHAKE clears your own bottom 3 rows, 2 SHIELD blocks the next attack, 3 FOG hides the top half of the other player's field for 5 s, 4 HANDS swaps their left and right for 5 s, 5 RUSH runs their game at double speed for 6 s.
- Fog, Hands and Rush work against every puzzle (they also work over the LAN).
- Arsenal's Empty Inventory empties a Power Brick player's meter and shield.
- Classic Power Brick rules from your checklist: no hold, ghost, hard drop, wall kicks or 7-bag; 5 next pieces, T-spins and garbage cancelling stay.
